Changelog

The releases worth reading about, newest first. The app applies updates on its own, and this page is here so you can see what changed and when.

v0.1.0

9 August 2026

Chat, presence and a space with every friend

  • Chat inside a call, kept with the call, so it is still there the next time you open it
  • Live presence for friends, including «waiting for you» when someone is sitting in a space you share
  • A private space with every friend, opened from their row or from a pin on the home screen
  • Friend requests and profile changes arrive without a refresh
  • Settings became a screen of its own

The way a call is joined changed, so installs older than 0.1.0 update themselves.

v0.0.15

7 August 2026

An honest camera

  • Turning the camera on now succeeds only once a camera has actually opened, so a missing or busy one says so instead of lighting the button and sending nothing
  • The camera button dims when there is no camera, and the keyboard shortcut follows the same rule

v0.0.14

7 August 2026

Screen sharing on Wayland

  • Sharing on Wayland goes through your desktop's own chooser and PipeWire, instead of looking as though it worked while everyone else saw a black rectangle. It took four attempts, from 0.0.9 to 0.0.13, and this is the release where it holds.
  • The chooser appears every time you start sharing, so you can pick a different window than last time
  • A session that cannot be captured at all refuses with a message, before anything reaches the call
  • On Linux the update button no longer appears minutes before the package reaches the repository

v0.0.8

5 August 2026

Package updates that say what they are doing

  • The Linux update started from inside the app shows progress, failure and the restart prompt, instead of looking as though nothing had happened for tens of seconds
  • The relaunch waits for the old process to exit, so a stalled shutdown no longer leaves you without a window
  • Both download buckets are pruned to the newest two versions

v0.0.7

5 August 2026

Linux window, icon and theme

  • The window has a title bar on GNOME again. The Wayland decoration plugin was missing from the packages, so the window came up bare and could not be moved.
  • The app icon shows up instead of a generic cog
  • A dark desktop no longer starts Callifornia in light mode on a fresh install
  • Tooltips wait longer before appearing

v0.0.5

5 August 2026

Callifornia on Linux

  • Packages in .deb and .rpm, with a signed APT and YUM repository for updates
  • Microphone through PulseAudio, camera through V4L2, screen sharing on X11
  • Updates in one click through your package manager
  • A redesigned home screen and navigation
  • Wayland screen sharing came later, in 0.0.9

v0.0.2

2 August 2026

A clean uninstall

  • Uninstalling removes the install directory, your data and the updater's leftover startup entry. New installs only.

v0.0.1

2 August 2026

First release

Hello, world

  • Group video meetings with short, shareable meeting IDs
  • Friends list, add people once and call them with one click
  • Invites inside the app, see who invited you and join right from there
  • Screen sharing with a picker for a window or the whole screen
  • Light and dark themes
  • Updates in the background, signed, only the changed files, applied on one restart
  • An installer for you alone on Windows 10/11, no administrator rights required
