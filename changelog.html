Changelog

The releases worth reading about, newest first. The app applies updates on its own, and this page is here so you can see what changed and when.

v0.2.1

23 August 2026

A moment before the door opens

  • A call now sets itself up before anyone can see or hear you. Camera and microphone are yours to decide on a screen with a live level meter, and a link opened outside the app always stops there first.
  • The safety number check became one card with the number split in two halves, instead of three paragraphs to read before anything could be compared
  • The key of a room travels with your call history, so a call taken on one computer opens on another
  • Invites that went missing, and invites that arrived under the wrong name
  • A brief connection hiccup no longer claims your session expired and puts you back on the home screen
  • Confirmation codes lost their dash, and the emails carrying them read correctly in a dark mailbox

v0.2.0

21 August 2026

Mandatory

Sealed on your side, and a computer that has to be let in

  • Messages, call audio and video, and the key to every room are sealed on your computer with keys the service never receives. What it stores is ciphertext it cannot open.
  • An account needs a confirmed address. You sign up with an email and a password, a code confirms the address, and your username comes from that address.
  • A computer this account has not used before signs in but opens nothing until a computer you already use reads an eight character code off it. With no other computer left, a code goes to your email instead.
  • Safety numbers, so you can be sure it is really them, and a notice on somebody's row the moment their key changes
  • A call history that belongs to the account rather than to one machine, sealed before it leaves, so all of your computers show the same list. It can be switched off or cleared, and calls older than thirty days go on their own.
  • Spaces and groups are gone. A call is a link, and the people you talk to live in one list.

Older installs update themselves, and conversations from before this release do not come across, because nothing that could carry them survived the move to keys the service does not hold.

v0.1.1

11 August 2026

The app is called Callifornia

  • Renamed from Calls, with a home of its own at callifornia.online
  • Russian throughout, chosen from the system the first time it runs and switchable in settings without a restart
  • The installer asks which language before it starts

This one arrives as a fresh install rather than an update. A copy of Calls already on the machine keeps working and stays where it is, so take the new installer from the home page.

v0.1.0

9 August 2026

Chat, presence and a space with every friend

  • Chat inside a call, kept with the call, so it is still there the next time you open it
  • Live presence for friends, including «waiting for you» when someone is sitting in a space you share
  • A private space with every friend, opened from their row or from a pin on the home screen
  • Friend requests and profile changes arrive without a refresh
  • Settings became a screen of its own

The way a call is joined changed, so installs older than 0.1.0 update themselves.

v0.0.15

7 August 2026

An honest camera

  • Turning the camera on now succeeds only once a camera has actually opened, so a missing or busy one says so instead of lighting the button and sending nothing
  • The camera button dims when there is no camera, and the keyboard shortcut follows the same rule

v0.0.14

7 August 2026

Screen sharing on Wayland

  • Sharing on Wayland goes through your desktop's own chooser and PipeWire, instead of looking as though it worked while everyone else saw a black rectangle. It took four attempts, from 0.0.9 to 0.0.13, and this is the release where it holds.
  • The chooser appears every time you start sharing, so you can pick a different window than last time
  • A session that cannot be captured at all refuses with a message, before anything reaches the call
  • On Linux the update button no longer appears minutes before the package reaches the repository

v0.0.8

5 August 2026

Package updates that say what they are doing

  • The Linux update started from inside the app shows progress, failure and the restart prompt, instead of looking as though nothing had happened for tens of seconds
  • The relaunch waits for the old process to exit, so a stalled shutdown no longer leaves you without a window
  • Both download buckets are pruned to the newest two versions

v0.0.7

5 August 2026

Linux window, icon and theme

  • The window has a title bar on GNOME again. The Wayland decoration plugin was missing from the packages, so the window came up bare and could not be moved.
  • The app icon shows up instead of a generic cog
  • A dark desktop no longer starts Callifornia in light mode on a fresh install
  • Tooltips wait longer before appearing

v0.0.5

5 August 2026

Callifornia on Linux

  • Packages in .deb and .rpm, with a signed APT and YUM repository for updates
  • Microphone through PulseAudio, camera through V4L2, screen sharing on X11
  • Updates in one click through your package manager
  • A redesigned home screen and navigation
  • Wayland screen sharing came later, in 0.0.9

v0.0.2

A clean uninstall

  • Uninstalling removes the install directory, your data and the updater's leftover startup entry. New installs only.

First release

Hello, world
