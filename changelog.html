Changelog

The releases worth reading about, newest first. The app applies updates on its own, and this page is here so you can see what changed and when.

v0.2.10

12 September 2026

A chat with every friend

  • Friends is a messenger now. Everyone you have added is on the left, and a chat with each of them opens on the right, whether or not anybody is calling. A call between the two of you shows the same chat
  • When a friend writes, a card in the corner says who and what, and a click on it opens the chat. What you have not read is counted on their row and on the Friends button, and the chat marks where it begins
  • You see when a friend is typing to you. A switch in Settings stops them seeing it from you
  • Messages come in bubbles with the face of whoever wrote them, and a wide window lines them all up on the left. A picture opens over the whole screen, and the mouse wheel moves a chat by lines rather than a few pixels
  • Mute a friend from the chat's menu. Their cards stop, their count turns grey, and it holds on every computer you use
  • Pinned friends fold into a row of faces on the home screen, and open into a list with a call and a chat button beside each name
  • The tile of a minimized call can be dragged anywhere in the window
  • The call history shows faces, the friend you called or the people in a meeting
  • Online is a blue dot now. The name and picture only you see for a friend moved into their profile, and friendship requests into a button over the list
  • Errors are said in the language of the app. Some came through as the server wrote them, in English, and a wrong password showed as a bare code
  • What the service keeps about you has changed, so the privacy policy has too. It now says that the service knows which friends keep a chat and when its messages were sent, that it passes on who is typing and keeps none of it, and that who you pinned and muted is sealed like the rest of your settings

v0.2.8

9 September 2026

How long ago somebody was here, and a switch to keep it to yourself

  • The friends list says how long ago a person was last in the app, instead of only that they are away
  • A switch in Settings hides that from everyone and leaves you simply offline. Whether you are online right now is not affected by it
  • Sound holds together on a patchy connection instead of breaking up
  • The sign-in and email fields say what to type instead of showing somebody else's address as an example
  • What the service keeps about you has changed, so the privacy policy has too. The part about presence now says that the time is written down, that your friends see it, and how to switch it off

v0.2.0

21 August 2026

Mandatory

Sealed on your side, and a computer that has to be let in

  • Messages, call audio and video, and the key to every room are sealed on your computer with keys the service never receives. What it stores is ciphertext it cannot open.
  • An account needs a confirmed address. You sign up with an email and a password, a code confirms the address, and your username comes from that address.
  • A computer this account has not used before signs in but opens nothing until a computer you already use reads an eight character code off it. With no other computer left, a code goes to your email instead.
  • Safety numbers, so you can be sure it is really them, and a notice on somebody's row the moment their key changes
  • A call history that belongs to the account rather than to one machine, sealed before it leaves, so all of your computers show the same list. It can be switched off or cleared, and calls older than thirty days go on their own.
  • Spaces and groups are gone. A call is a link, and the people you talk to live in one list.

Older installs update themselves, and conversations from before this release do not come across, because nothing that could carry them survived the move to keys the service does not hold.

Screen sharing on Wayland

  • Sharing on Wayland goes through your desktop's own chooser and PipeWire, instead of looking as though it worked while everyone else saw a black rectangle. It took four attempts, from 0.0.9 to 0.0.13, and this is the release where it holds.
